## Best Practices for Outreach

### Timing
- **Best days:** Tuesday-Thursday
- **Best time:** 10am-2pm (avoid opening/closing rush)
- **Avoid:** Weekends (they're busy with customers)
- **Follow up:** Once after 1 week, then let it go

### Personalization
- Always use shop owner's name if you have it
- Reference something specific about their shop
- Mention if you've actually visited
- Acknowledge their specialty/expertise

### Response Rates
- Initial listing email: 15-25% response rate
- Monthly stats: 40-60% open rate
- Feature request: 30-40% response rate
- Paid ad offer: 5-10% conversion (only send when traffic is strong)

### What NOT to Do
- ❌ Don't spam them weekly
- ❌ Don't make claims about results you can't back up
- ❌ Don't be pushy about paid features
- ❌ Don't list them without permission if they request removal
- ❌ Don't share their stats publicly without permission

### Tracking
Keep a simple spreadsheet:
- Shop name
- Owner name
- Email sent date
- Response (Y/N)
- Claimed (Y/N)
- Notes

### Common Responses

**"How much does this cost?"**
```
It's completely free! We make money from ads and premium features, but basic listings are always free.
```

**"I'm too busy to manage this"**
```
No worries - your listing is already live with the basic info. You don't have to do anything unless you want to add more photos or details. We'll just send you monthly stats emails.
```

**"Remove my listing"**
```
No problem at all! I've removed your listing. If you ever change your mind, just let me know. Thanks for your time!
```

**"What's the catch?"**
```
No catch! We're trying to build the best resource for vintage collectors. Free shop listings help us do that. We make money from ads and optional premium features, but you never have to pay anything.
```
